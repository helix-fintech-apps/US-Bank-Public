Transfers between your accounts

Move money now, on a date you choose, or every week or month. Transfers between USA Banc accounts are instant.

USA Pay

Send money to friends and family with just their email or phone number, request money, and split a bill. Only send money to people you know and trust. (Our own person-to-person service in this demo; not connected to any outside payment network.)

Link an external account (ACH)

Link an account at another bank, confirm two small deposits, and move money in or out. Transfers usually take 1–3 business days.

Wire transfers

Send large amounts the same business day when you send before the cut-off time. Wires can't be canceled once sent.

Bill pay

Add your billers once, then pay them on a schedule or one time. See when each payment will arrive.

Card autopay

Pay your USA Banc credit card automatically: the minimum, the statement balance, or a fixed amount.

Mobile check deposit

Take a photo of the front and back of a check. Most deposits are available the next business day. (Coming soon in this demo.)
